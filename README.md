# Progress Tracker

A gentle little corner of the internet that remembers your streaks, cheers you on, and never once makes you feel behind.

Pick a subject, start the clock, and let this tracker hold onto your streaks, your sessions, and your small wins — one quiet minute at a time. No pressure, no guilt, just you showing up for yourself.

## What it does

- **Stopwatch** — pick a topic and sub-topic, hit start, and watch the time add up in real time.
- **Subjects & sub-topics** — organize what you're studying, tag it, and set a target (from "get acquainted" all the way to "become an expert").
- **Streaks & stats** — see your current streak, total sessions, and overall progress across everything you're working on.
- **Mood check-ins** — a quick "how are you feeling" before and after each session, because how you *feel* matters as much as how long you studied.
- **Session reflection** — a short, friendly quiz after each session that tags it as Deep work, Review, or Practice.
- **Last 7 days chart** — a simple bar chart so you can see your rhythm at a glance.
- **Daily notes** — a sticky-note style space for quick thoughts, what clicked, and what to pick up tomorrow.
- **Light & dark mode** — matches your system by default, with a one-click toggle.

## Getting started

This is a single, self-contained HTML file — no build step, no installs, no dependencies.

1. Download `study-tracker.html` from this repo.
2. Open it in any browser.
3. Start studying.

Your data (subjects, sessions, notes) is saved right in your browser, so it'll be there the next time you open the page.

### Running it as a live site

If you'd like a shareable link instead of a local file, turn on **GitHub Pages** for this repo (Settings → Pages → source: `main` branch), and it'll be live at:

```
https://vaishnaviarni.github.io/study-progress-tracker/study-tracker.html
```
